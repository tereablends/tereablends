### About

We're the guides team behind [TEREA Blends](https://iqosblends.ae/), an independent heated tobacco retailer in Dubai. We write plain-language explainers on how IQOS devices work and which sticks fit which device, for adult users across the UAE.

Our work answers three questions people ask most:

- **Which sticks fit my device?** TEREA works only in IQOS ILUMA. BLENDS works only in BONDS by IQOS. HEETS works only in older blade-based IQOS devices.
- **What does this light mean?** Status lights and touchscreen signs on IQOS ILUMA, ILUMA i and BONDS, with the official reset steps.
- **How do the models differ?** ILUMA i, ILUMA i One and ILUMA i Prime, the previous ILUMA generation, and BONDS by IQOS, compared on design, battery and features.

<img src="assets/divider.svg" width="100%" alt="">

### How we work

- **Sourced facts only.** Every device spec and light meaning comes from an official IQOS page or user manual, and we record the source for each entry.
- **No invented numbers.** If a value can't be confirmed, we leave it out and say so.
- **Kept current.** Product details come from the live store catalog, so they stay in step with what's actually available.

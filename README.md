## Which sticks fit which IQOS device

The three IQOS stick families look alike, but each works in one type of device only. A stick in the wrong device won't heat.

| Stick family | Works in | How it's heated |
|---|---|---|
| **TEREA** | IQOS ILUMA only: ILUMA i, ILUMA i One, ILUMA i Prime, and the earlier ILUMA, ILUMA One and ILUMA Prime | SMARTCORE induction: a coil in the device heats a metal strip sealed inside each TEREA stick |
| **BLENDS** | BONDS by IQOS only | ROUNDHEAT: a heater around the stick warms the tobacco from the outside |
| **HEETS** | Older blade-based IQOS devices only, such as the IQOS 3 DUO and IQOS ORIGINALS DUO | A heated blade pushes into the tobacco |

**Why they don't cross over:** HEETS have no metal strip, so an ILUMA has nothing to heat. BLENDS have no metal strip either, because BONDS heats from the outside. TEREA is designed for ILUMA's induction system, not for a blade.

**Safety:** don't swallow or take apart TEREA sticks. They contain a sharp metal part. Keep them away from children.

## Which IQOS do I have?

The same light can mean different things on different models, so identify yours first:

- **A holder in a separate pocket charger, with a touchscreen on the charger:** IQOS ILUMA i, often called ILUMA i Standard. If the charger wraps around the holder, it's ILUMA i Prime.
- **A single piece with a strip of lights and no screen:** IQOS ILUMA i One.
- **A holder in a pocket charger with status lights but no touchscreen:** a first-generation ILUMA or ILUMA Prime.
- **A single piece with status lights, taking TEREA:** a first-generation ILUMA One.
- **A compact single piece that uses vibrations and takes BLENDS:** BONDS by IQOS.
- **A holder with a blade inside that pushes into the stick:** an older IQOS such as the IQOS 3 DUO or ORIGINALS DUO.

## IQOS ILUMA i vs ILUMA i One vs ILUMA i Prime

All three current ILUMA i models heat TEREA the same way, with SMARTCORE induction and no blade to clean. They differ in design, battery setup and features. The base model, IQOS ILUMA i, is also widely searched as **ILUMA i Standard**.

| | ILUMA i (Standard) | ILUMA i One | ILUMA i Prime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design | Holder plus pocket charger | One piece, all-in-one | Holder plus wrap-around pocket charger |
| Sessions in a row | Up to 3 (Performance Mode) | Up to 20 | Up to 3 (Performance Mode) |
| Sessions per full charge | Up to 20 | Up to 20 | Up to 20 |
| Full charge time | About 135 min (pocket charger) | About 90 min | About 135 min (pocket charger) |
| Preheat | About 20 s | About 20 s | About 20 s |
| Session length | Up to 6 min or 14 puffs | Up to 6 min or 14 puffs | Up to 6 min or 14 puffs |
| Touchscreen | Yes | No (strip lights) | Yes |
| Pause Mode | Yes | No | Yes |
| FlexBattery | Yes | Not listed by IQOS | Yes |
| FlexPuff | Yes, up to 4 extra puffs | Yes | Yes, up to 4 extra puffs |
| Weight | Holder 31 g, charger 116 g | Figures vary | Holder 31 g, charger 134 g |

**Which one fits how you use it:**
- **ILUMA i One** if you want one light device and the most sessions back to back.
- **ILUMA i (Standard)** if you want the touchscreen, Pause Mode and FlexBattery.
- **ILUMA i Prime** if you want those same features in the premium wrap-around design.

**Pause Mode** (ILUMA i and i Prime) lets you stop a session and resume within 8 minutes. **FlexBattery** lets you choose Performance Mode, for up to 3 sessions in a row, or Eco Mode, one session per holder charge, which IQOS says can extend the holder battery's lifespan.

## First-generation ILUMA, ILUMA One and ILUMA Prime

The first ILUMA generation is still in use across the UAE. All three models take TEREA, use SMARTCORE induction, preheat in about 20 seconds and give up to 20 sessions per full charge.

- **IQOS ILUMA and ILUMA Prime:** holder plus pocket charger. The pocket charger takes about 2 hours 15 minutes to charge fully. The holder recharges in about 1 minute 50 seconds for one session.
- **IQOS ILUMA One:** all-in-one, with up to 20 sessions in a row and a full charge in about 1 hour 30 minutes.
- **Sessions in a row on ILUMA and ILUMA Prime:** most IQOS pages say 2. One IQOS comparison page says up to 3.
- **No touchscreen, Pause Mode or FlexBattery.** Those arrived with the ILUMA i generation.

## BONDS by IQOS

BONDS by IQOS is a compact, all-in-one device that uses BLENDS sticks only. The current model is BONDS 2 by IQOS, with:

- **Up to 28 sessions** per full charge (varies with use and stick type)
- **Up to 3 sessions in a row,** then an automatic cool-down
- **About 30 seconds** of preheating
- **About 120 minutes** to charge fully
- **No blade and no routine cleaning**

**A normal BONDS session:** the first vibration means preheating has started and the status lights pulse. The second vibration, about 30 seconds later, means the device is ready. One vibration with one pulsing white light means about 30 seconds are left. After the third session in a row, two waves of running lights show BONDS is cooling down.

## IQOS light meanings

The most common status lights and touchscreen signs, with the fix. If a problem continues after a reset, contact IQOS customer care.

| Device | What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| ILUMA i, ILUMA i Prime | 2 vertical lines (top and bottom), alternating 3 times | Outside the 0 to 40 °C operating range | Bring the device back to room temperature |
| ILUMA i, ILUMA i Prime | Letter "E" flashing 3 times | Device error | Reset the device, or update the firmware in the IQOS app |
| ILUMA i, ILUMA i Prime | Horizontal line with a circle below | Device is locked | Turn off Device Lock in the IQOS app |
| ILUMA i, ILUMA i Prime | 1 or 2 horizontal lines flashing 8 times | Holder battery life is over | Check replacement eligibility with IQOS |
| ILUMA i One | Top and bottom lights flash white on a short press | Device Lock is on | IQOS app, select the device, Device settings, Lock device, switch it off |
| ILUMA i One | Red light | Possible firmware issue | Check for the latest firmware, then reset |
| ILUMA i One | Yellow or orange light | No confirmed meaning for this model | Charge fully; if it stays, contact IQOS customer care |
| ILUMA (first generation) | Red light on the holder or pocket charger | Malfunction, or an interrupted firmware update | Reset: holder in the pocket charger, hold the button 10 s until the lights fade out and back in |
| ILUMA (first generation) | Yellow light on the holder | Holder battery depleted | Put the holder back in the pocket charger |
| ILUMA (first generation) | Holder white lights blink twice | Outside the 0 to 40 °C range | Let it cool down or warm up |
| ILUMA One (first generation) | Red blinking light | Reset needed | Hold the button 10 s, then release |
| BONDS by IQOS | Red blinking light | Reset needed | Reset; all lights turn off and back on to confirm |
| BONDS 2 by IQOS | Two waves of running status lights | Cool-down after 3 sessions in a row | Wait a few minutes before the next session |

## How to reset an IQOS

For a red light, a reset is the first fix.

- **ILUMA and ILUMA Prime (first generation):** put the holder in the pocket charger, hold the pocket charger's button for about 10 seconds, and release when the lights fade out, fade back in, blink twice and the device vibrates.
- **ILUMA One (first generation):** hold the button for 10 seconds, then release. All the lights turn off and back on.
- **ILUMA i One:** when a reset completes, the lights fade in, blink twice quickly, ramp up and show the battery level.
- **ILUMA i and ILUMA i Prime:** if the screen shows the "E" error, reset the device or update its firmware in the IQOS app.
- **BONDS by IQOS:** the reset is complete after the third vibration, when all the status lights turn off and come back on.

If the same error returns after a reset, update the firmware in the IQOS app before contacting customer care.

## Battery level lights

- **ILUMA i One:** four lights mean more than 75% charge, and one light means less than 25%.
- **First-generation ILUMA pocket charger:** three white lights mean 50 to 75%, two mean 25 to 50%, and one means less than 25%. A yellow strip light means the charger battery is low.
- **First-generation ILUMA holder:** a yellow light means the holder has no sessions left. Put it back in the pocket charger.
- **BONDS by IQOS:** after a reset, one light shows 25%, two show 50% and three show 75%.

Whatever the model, charge the device fully before treating a light as a fault.

## TEREA by origin in Dubai

TEREA is made for many countries, and each market gets its own version. The origin is the market the pack was made for. The pack design, health warnings and flavor line-up change between origins, but **every TEREA fits every ILUMA device.**

IQOS Blends stocks eight market versions:

- **Indonesia:** 24 flavors, including nine Edition sticks made with Indonesian clove
- **Japan:** 23 flavors, including the Regular tobacco family and the Black menthol series
- **Uzbekistan:** 20 flavors, a good origin for uncooled tobacco such as Teak, Sienna and Russet. See [TEREA Uzbekistan](https://iqosblends.ae/terea/origin/uzbekistan).
- **Kazakhstan:** 15 flavors, mostly medium strength, with several Pearl capsule sticks
- **Italy:** 11 flavors
- **Kyrgyzstan:** 9 flavors
- **Switzerland:** 8 flavors
- **Tajikistan:** 5 flavors

**Same name, different notes:** some flavor names exist in several origins with different listed notes or ratings. If you have a favorite, check the origin before you reorder.

## Using IQOS in the Dubai heat

IQOS lists an operating range of **0 to 40 °C** for ILUMA. A Dubai summer passes that easily, especially inside a parked car.

- **Keep the device with you,** not in the glovebox or on the dashboard.
- **Charge indoors,** away from direct sun.
- **If it won't start after sitting somewhere hot,** let it return to room temperature indoors. Don't put it in the fridge.
- **Keep cartons sealed** and stored away from direct sunlight until you need them.

## FAQ

**Can I use TEREA in BONDS by IQOS?**
No. TEREA is made for IQOS ILUMA. BONDS works only with BLENDS.

**Can I use HEETS in IQOS ILUMA?**
No. HEETS have no metal strip, so ILUMA can't heat them.

**Does TEREA from Japan or Indonesia work in an ILUMA bought in Dubai?**
Yes. Every TEREA origin fits every ILUMA device.

**What's the difference between ILUMA i and ILUMA i Prime?**
Only the design. Both have the same holder, touchscreen, Pause Mode, FlexBattery and battery figures. ILUMA i Prime has a wrap-around pocket charger.

**What's the difference between ILUMA i and ILUMA i One?**
ILUMA i One is one piece and gives up to 20 sessions in a row, with no touchscreen or Pause Mode. ILUMA i is a holder in a pocket charger, with up to 3 sessions in a row from the holder, plus the touchscreen, Pause Mode and FlexBattery.

**Does IQOS ILUMA need cleaning?**
Not inside. There's no blade, and the tobacco stays sealed in the stick. Wipe the outside with a soft, dry cloth.

**Why won't my IQOS start on a hot day?**
IQOS lists a 0 to 40 °C operating range. Let the device cool to room temperature indoors and try again.

**Why won't my BONDS start a fourth stick?**
BONDS gives up to 3 sessions in a row, then cools down automatically. Two waves of running lights show it's cooling. Wait a few minutes.

**What does a red light on an IQOS mean?**
Usually that the device needs a reset. On ILUMA i and ILUMA i Prime, the equivalent warning is an "E" flashing on the touchscreen.
